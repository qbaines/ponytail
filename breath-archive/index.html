Breath Archive

What did historical writers mean by breath, practice, and healing? We read digitized texts closely, put them in context, and separate historical beliefs from modern medical evidence.

Not medical advice. These essays describe historical ideas, not treatments or instructions. They do not recommend replacing medical care or attempting strenuous breath practices.

How we work

Every article starts with an identifiable text and edition. We distinguish the date of an ancient work from the date of its translation or scan, link readers to the source, and label modern research separately. We do not treat an old healing claim as proof that a practice works.

Breath Archive is an independent educational project. Historical traditions are living traditions; our summaries are not a substitute for reading the source or consulting qualified practitioners and clinicians.
